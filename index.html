ABOUT THE CONFERENCE

Shaping Resilient Futures Through Research

The 19th International Research Conference of the Faculty of Architecture Research Unit, University of Moratuwa, brings together researchers, academics, practitioners and industry professionals to explore innovative ideas for the future of the built environment.

Under the theme "Resilient Intelligence: Adapting the Built Environment to a Changing World", FARU 2026 creates a platform for knowledge sharing, interdisciplinary collaboration and research excellence.
